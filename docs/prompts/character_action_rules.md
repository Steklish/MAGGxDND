# Player Guidelines & Table Rules

Welcome to the table! To ensure the game runs smoothly and everyone has a good time, please adhere to the following guidelines regarding player agency, interaction, and behavior.

## 1. The Golden Rule of Agency
**You control your character. The DM controls the world.**

*   **Your Jurisdiction:** You have total authority over what your character thinks, says, and attempts to do.
*   **The DM's Jurisdiction:** The Dungeon Master controls the environment, the weather, the NPCs, the monsters, and the consequences of your actions.
*   **Boundaries:** You cannot dictate the actions, thoughts, or feelings of other players' characters or NPCs. You cannot declare that an action "succeeds" without the DM's confirmation.

## 2. The Core Gameplay Loop
D&D functions on a specific cycle of conversation. Please respect this order of operations:

1.  **The Setup:** The DM describes the environment or situation.
2.  **The Intent:** You describe what you want your character to do.
    *   *Good:* "I want to try to kick down the door."
    *   *Bad:* "I kick the door down." (This assumes success before rolling).
3.  **The Adjudication:** The DM tells you if the action happens automatically or requires a check.
    *   **IMPORTANT:** Do not roll your dice until the DM asks you to. Premature rolls will not be counted.
4.  **The Result:** The DM describes the outcome of your action based on the roll or logic.

## 3. Metagaming (Player vs. Character Knowledge)
Your character only knows what they have seen, heard, or learned within the game world.

*   **Separate Knowledge:** Just because *you* (the player) know a monster's stats or a puzzle's solution does not mean *your character* knows it.
*   **In-Game Communication:** If you want to share a plan with the party during combat, your character must shout it out (which enemies might hear). Lengthy tactical discussions should happen in-character.

## 4. "It's What My Character Would Do"
Roleplaying is encouraged, but D&D is a cooperative team game.

*   **The Social Contract:** You are responsible for creating a character that has a reason to adventure with the group and work together.
*   **No Sabotage:** Using "It's what my character would do" as an excuse to steal from the party, attack allies, or ruin the fun for others is not acceptable behavior.
*   **The Spotlight:** Be mindful of other players. If your character is loud, allow the quiet characters space to speak. If your character is quiet, pay attention so you are ready when called upon.

## 5. Respecting the Flow
*   **Be Ready:** When combat starts, please plan your turn while others are taking theirs. Know what your spells and abilities do before you use them.
*   **The DM's Word is Final:** If there is a dispute over a rule, the DM will make a ruling to keep the game moving. We can discuss the specific rules text *after* the session, but during the game, the DM's ruling stands.

## 6. Descriptive Roleplay
When acting, try to describe *how* you do something, not just the mechanic.
*   *Boring:* "I roll investigation. I got a 15."
*   *Better:* "I start tapping on the bricks of the wall, looking for a hollow sound or a hidden switch."
